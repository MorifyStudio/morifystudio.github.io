Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Request account and data deletion

1. Who is responsible

Morify Studio, also shown as Morify Apps in Google Play, provides Frontier Bastion. This policy covers the game, its wallet service and this support page. Contact: morifystudio@gmail.com.

2. Game and wallet data

Settings, saved matches and formations are stored on your device. Online features use a randomly generated account ID and a secure session token. Our Cloudflare-hosted service stores Gold balances, transactions, rewards, mission progress and purchase rights. A recovery code is stored on the server only as a cryptographic hash. Do not send anyone your session token or recovery code. We do not request contacts, precise GPS location, microphone, camera or your photo library.

3. Purchases and service providers

Google Play or Apple processes payments; we do not receive full card numbers. Purchase IDs, product IDs, receipts or purchase tokens are used to verify delivery, restore rights, process refunds and prevent duplicate grants. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous game account ID, purchase information and technical SDK/device information for purchase management. Cloudflare processes requests and IP addresses for hosting and security. GitHub Pages hosts this policy. These providers process data under their own policies.

4. Advertising and choices

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process advertising/device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics. Ads can use device storage. The game requests contextual/non-personalized ads and limits ad content for a teen audience; this does not mean that no data is processed. Regional consent and privacy choices are presented where applicable. You can revisit Ad privacy in the game and use your device’s advertising controls. Rewarded ads are optional; verified reward records prevent repeated claims.

5. Optional statistics and purposes

Gameplay statistics sharing is off by default. If enabled, limited events such as map starts/results, store views and reward outcomes are associated with the game account ID. They help measure reliability, balance and the experience. Turn the option off to stop future optional events. Core wallet and security processing continues because it is needed to provide the service. We use data for providing the game, security and fraud prevention, legal obligations, and consent-based processing where required. We do not sell wallet records or recovery codes.

6. Retention and security

Optional event records are kept for approximately 90 days with daily cleanup. Device saves remain until removed or the app is deleted, subject to operating-system backups. Online account data is kept while needed to operate the account. Limited purchase and anti-fraud records may remain after deletion where necessary for legal obligations, refund handling and preventing reuse of transactions. Data travels over HTTPS; access tokens are kept in protected device storage. No service can promise absolute security. Providers may process data in other countries under applicable safeguards.

7. Deletion and privacy rights

Use the in-game account/data deletion request or email morifystudio@gmail.com with subject “Frontier Bastion — deletion request”. Include your game account ID if available; never include a recovery code, password or card details. We may request proportionate proof of ownership. We remove or anonymize account, progress and optional statistics data after verification, except the limited records explained above. Deletion can permanently remove Gold and progress; requesting deletion does not automatically request a store refund. For local saves, use the game reset or device app-data controls. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority.

8. Age, support and changes

The intended audience is age 13 and over; the game is not directed to children under 13. Do not use online features if you are below this age. If you believe a child provided data, contact us. A parent or guardian may assist with a privacy request. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date and, where required, an in-game notice or renewed choice. Contact us before sending sensitive information.
